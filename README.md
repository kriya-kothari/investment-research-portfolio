# Investment Research Portfolio

This repository contains my investment research projects, including company valuations, financial models, and equity research reports.

## Overview

The goal of this project is to develop a consistent framework for analyzing public companies across different industries. Each case study follows the same research process, from understanding the business and industry to building a DCF valuation model and forming an investment recommendation.

Each project includes:

- Business & Industry Analysis
- Historical Financial Analysis
- Forecast Assumptions
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation
- Investment Memo
- Equity Research Report

## Research Methodology

1. Business Understanding
2. Industry & Competitive Analysis
3. Historical Financial Analysis
4. Forecasting & Assumptions
5. DCF Valuation
6. Investment Memo
7. Equity Research Report
